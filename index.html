Owlcheck privacy policy

Know the phone before you pay.

Effective 20 November 2026

Owlcheck tests the hardware of the phone it runs on and makes a report you can share. It has no accounts and no server of its own. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain words.

What stays on your phone

  • Test results and reports. The phone's model, maker, Android version, RAM, storage size and the result of each test are saved on the phone so you can see past reports in History. They are never sent to us. Delete them at any time in History (one report or all), or by uninstalling the app.
  • Camera. The camera test shows a live picture so you can judge it. Nothing is photographed, recorded or saved.
  • Microphone. The microphone test records 3 seconds of sound, plays it back once, and then discards it. It is never saved or sent.
  • Location. The GPS test uses your location only to count satellites while the test is on screen. Your location is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Other sensors and radios. Sensor readings, battery information, and whether Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are on are read only during the tests. The app doesn't scan for or connect to networks or devices.

Owlcheck never reads your contacts, messages, photos, files, phone number, IMEI, accounts or call history.

Each permission is asked for only when the test that needs it starts, and you can refuse. A refused permission only means that test is marked "Not tested".

Sharing a report

A report leaves your phone only when you tap Share image or Share PDF and choose an app (for example WhatsApp or Gmail). It then goes where you send it. Reports contain the phone's model and test results, and no personal information.

Ads

Owlcheck is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home and history screens, at most one full-screen ad after a full check, and an optional video ad to unlock the PDF report (the image report is always free). No ads are shown before your first full check is finished.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • app interactions with ads and diagnostics (such as crash information from the ads SDK),
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language).

Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Apart from what the ads SDK collects and crash reports (below), no information leaves your phone.

Crash reports

If Owlcheck crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the problem can be fixed. A crash report contains technical details only: the error and where in the app it happened, the phone model, Android version, app version, free memory and storage at the time, and a random installation ID that isn't linked to you. It never contains your test results, reports, recordings, pictures or location. Google's terms for Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Children

Owlcheck is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.
